# Grenen — Statics Round 2 (45 concepts)

**Format:** 4:5 (1080 × 1350) · **Brand:** persillo · **Product:** Grenen · **Engine:** gemini-image (Nano Banana / Pro)

Brings round-1 learnings forward:
- Form-constraints baked into prompt_builder (cylinder, flat top, 1 wick, exactly 4 per pack)
- Mixed concepts now pass BOTH Gylden + Råhvid hero refs
- Råhvid variant description hardened (cream-ivory, never pure white)
- Copy follows 4 rules (write the system, outside-in, Eugene Schwartz, autentisk dansk)

**Diversification axes** (per Andromeda):
- Templates: 18+ distinct competitor refs
- Variants: Gylden 15 / Råhvid 15 / Mixed 15 — balanced
- Copy mode: embedded heavy 10, embedded light 8, annotated 5, text-free 22
- USP focus: 14t · ren bivoks · Bland Selv · hygge · objekt · gave · scene
- Mood: morgen, aften, vinter, gave, hverdag, dinner, gift
